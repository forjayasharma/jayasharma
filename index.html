Jaya Sharma

PhD student in Cybersecurity Engineering at the DTU Cryptography Group, Technical University of Denmark, supervised by Christian Majenz.

Research

My research sits at the intersection of theoretical cryptography and quantum computing. I study the security of cryptographic constructions in the quantum random oracle model (QROM) — how classical proof techniques hold up, and where they need to be rebuilt, once an adversary is allowed quantum access to a hash function.

More broadly, I'm interested in public-key cryptography, zero-knowledge proofs, lattice-based cryptography, and quantum information.

Before DTU, I completed a Master's in Mathematics at IIT Roorkee and a Bachelor's (Honours) in Mathematics at Lady Shri Ram College, University of Delhi, and spent three semesters as a Junior Research Fellow at IIIT Delhi.
